= Themes
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A theme gives a product its look.
These rules set what a theme may contain, and describe the three themes of {abbr-fpsot-first}.
The link:https://www.designtokens.org/tr/2025.10/resolver/[Design Tokens Resolver Module 2025.10] shows one way to merge token sets for a theme.

== What a theme contains

[[STY-050]]
*STY-050.* A theme MUST contain only color, type and layout.
Color and type are the values of the color and type tokens, and the font files the theme bundles.
Layout is the values of the spacing, radius, elevation and layout tokens, and where the page regions go.
Motion tokens are not part of a theme.
A theme MAY hold more than one color scheme, such as light, dark and high contrast.
With a clear limit, anyone can check that a theme changes the look and nothing else.

[[STY-051]]
*STY-051.* A theme MUST NOT change behavior.
It MUST NOT contain scripts or templates.
It MUST NOT change the states, keyboard use or focus order of a component.
It MUST NOT change validation, messages or confirmation steps.
It MUST NOT hide, add or reorder the content of a component.
People then get the same safe behavior everywhere, and the tests of the behavior hold for every theme.

[[STY-052]]
*STY-052.* A theme MUST NOT contain official marks, such as logos, wordmarks, signatures, flags or coats of arms.
The engine MUST give the header and the footer a place for marks.
The government body that deploys the product adds its own marks there.
Each government restricts the use of its marks, and only the body knows which marks it may show.

== The FPSOT default theme

[[STY-053]]
*STY-053.* The {abbr-fpsot} default theme MUST be link:https://www.patternfly.org/foundations-and-styles/design-tokens/overview[PatternFly] 6 as published, with its own token values.
It maps the {abbr-fpsot} semantic tokens to PatternFly semantic tokens.
It changes no value, except the overrides that STY-064 allows.
It MUST state the PatternFly version it maps.
Version 0.1 of this specification maps link:https://github.com/patternfly/patternfly/releases/tag/v6.7.0[PatternFly 6.7.0], released 2026-10-15.
The default theme suits staff and administration screens, and public services SHOULD use the theme of their jurisdiction.

== The Québec theme

[[STY-054]]
*STY-054.* The Québec theme MUST follow the link:https://design.quebec.ca/a-propos/systeme-design[Système de design gouvernemental] of Québec.
It takes its color palettes, its fonts, its 8-pixel grid and its shadow levels.
It MUST also follow the layout of the link:https://design.quebec.ca/design/modeles/bandeau-en-tete-piv[header banner] of the Québec visual identity program.
The body adds the government signature to that banner, as STY-052 sets.
Strict use of the design system is mandatory for Québec ministries and agencies.

== The Canada theme

[[STY-055]]
*STY-055.* The Canada theme MUST follow the link:https://design-system.canada.ca/en/[GC Design System] and the link:https://design.canada.ca/specifications.html[Canada.ca specifications].
It takes the design tokens of the GC Design System, and the mandatory colors, typography and layouts of Canada.ca.
It MUST follow the layout of the Canada.ca global header and footer.
The institution adds the federal signature and wordmark, as STY-052 sets.
The Canada.ca design is mandatory for the public sites of the federal institutions in its scope.

== Government behavior rules

[[STY-056]]
*STY-056.* A government design system can also set behavior, such as when to validate a field.
A theme MUST NOT carry that behavior, even when it follows that design system.
A binding government rule can set a behavior that differs from a rule of this specification.
The product MUST then record each such difference in its documentation.
The comparison pages of this specification show the known differences.
The product then states which rules it meets, and the theme stays a pure look.
