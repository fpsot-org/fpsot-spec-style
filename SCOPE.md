# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT style specification.

The specification covers the visual look of the screens of FPSOT products: design tokens and their format, tiers and names; the categories of tokens for color, type, spacing, radius, elevation, motion and layout; the states of interactive components; validation messages, loading indicators and empty states; the confirmation of destructive actions; and themes, including what a theme may contain, the FPSOT default, Québec and Canada themes, and how to check a theme against the FPSOT accessibility specification.

It does not set accessibility values, which belong to the FPSOT accessibility specification, nor the wording of text, which belongs to the FPSOT writing specification. It does not cover the official marks of any government.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
